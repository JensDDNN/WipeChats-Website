WipeChats helps you find older ChatGPT conversations, review your selection, and clean up your history in a controlled way.

How it worksPrivacy Policy
01 · FIND

Find

Find older conversations based on their actual update date.

02 · REVIEW

Review

Check your selection and, where available, view a short content preview.

03 · CLEAN UP

Clean Up

Explicitly confirm which conversations should be permanently removed.

04 · VERIFY

Verify

Where technically possible, check whether the removal succeeded.

CONTROL BY DESIGN

Safe through clear limits and explicit choices.

WipeChats uses a 30-day minimum age by default. You can also set a maximum number of conversations per action, skip project conversations, and optionally include archived conversations.

Important: deletion is permanent. Always review your selection before confirming.
