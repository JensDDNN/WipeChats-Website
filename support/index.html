Using WipeChats

1. Scan

Open WipeChats while you are signed in to ChatGPT, set your filters, and choose SCAN ALL CONVERSATIONS.

2. Select

Check conversations individually or use the selection control. The default minimum age is 30 days.

3. Review

Use REVIEW IN CHATGPT to check your selection before deleting.

4. Delete

Deletion is performed only after explicit confirmation. Deletion is permanent.

5. Verify

Where technically possible, WipeChats checks whether the conversation no longer appears in the available conversation list.

Frequently asked questions

Why can't I delete conversations younger than 30 days?

WipeChats intentionally uses a 30-day minimum age to keep recent conversations out of cleanup actions.

Can I skip project conversations?

Yes, using the project conversation filter.

Can I include archived conversations?

Yes, when you enable the option and those conversations are available to the extension.

Are conversations deleted automatically?

No. You select, review, and explicitly confirm the deletion.

What happens if verification fails?

WipeChats is designed not to continue blindly when a deletion cannot be verified.

Report a problem

Tell us what you tried to do, which step failed, and what message you saw. If possible, include a screenshot without sensitive information.
