Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how WipeChats handles information when you use the browser extension.

In short: WipeChats is an independent extension for finding, reviewing, and removing older ChatGPT conversations. WipeChats does not operate a cloud database that stores your chat history.

1. Who is responsible?

WipeChats is independent third-party software. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by OpenAI. Privacy questions: info@domela.eu.

2. What information does WipeChats process?

To provide its functionality, WipeChats may process information from your ChatGPT conversation history, including:

  • the conversation identifier;
  • the conversation title;
  • the date and time of the latest update;
  • information needed to distinguish project and archived conversations;
  • limited content from a selected conversation when needed to provide a short review preview.

3. Why is it used?

This information is used for the core WipeChats functions: finding, filtering, selecting, reviewing, deleting, and, where technically possible, verifying deletion.

4. No WipeChats cloud database

WipeChats does not operate its own server or database for collecting your chat history and does not create a separate online copy of your complete history. The extension operates in your browser and uses the available ChatGPT web functionality for the required actions.

5. Sharing and advertising

WipeChats does not sell, rent, or share your chat data with advertisers or data brokers and does not use chat data for personalized advertising. The ChatGPT service itself is subject to the terms and privacy policy of that service.

6. Local settings

Settings such as filter choices may be stored locally in your browser. WipeChats does not use them to build an online user profile.

7. Deletion and verification

After you explicitly confirm a deletion, WipeChats performs the removal. It then attempts, where technically possible, to verify that the conversation no longer appears in the available conversation list. WipeChats cannot restore a deleted conversation.

8. Security and passwords

WipeChats does not ask for your ChatGPT password and does not require a separate WipeChats account.

9. Retention

WipeChats does not maintain an online archive of your chat history.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated when functionality or applicable requirements change. The date above identifies the current version.

11. Contact

info@domela.eu

12. Chrome Web Store

WipeChats has one clear purpose: helping users find, review, and clean up their ChatGPT conversation history in a controlled way. Information use is limited to what is necessary for that purpose.
